Accessibility statement

We believe in equal service for everyone and work to make this website accessible to people with disabilities.

This website has been made accessible in accordance with the Israeli Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013, and Israeli Standard IS 5568, based on the WCAG 2.0 guidelines at level AA.

What we have done

  • Structured headings and defined navigation regions for comfortable screen-reader use.
  • Full keyboard navigation with a clear focus indicator and a "Skip to main content" link.
  • Text alternatives for meaningful images; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • Colour contrast at level AA, and layouts adapted for phone, tablet and desktop.
  • Forms with a label for every field and clear error messages.
  • Animations stop automatically for users who set "reduce motion" on their device, and can be stopped at any time from the accessibility menu. The moving logo strip also stops on hover or when "Stop animations" is selected.
  • An accessibility menu (screen corner): larger text, high contrast, highlighted links, readable font and stop animations.

Parts not yet fully accessible

Despite our efforts, some components may not yet be fully accessible, such as:

  • Third-party content – links to social networks and WhatsApp.
  • The moving logo strip on the home and clients pages. You can stop it with the "Pause motion" button below it or from the accessibility menu.
  • The interactive house demo at the top of the home page. All of its information also appears on the service pages, and it can be used with a keyboard.

If you encounter a problem, please let us know.

Accessibility at our offices

The Company has no office open to the public. Service is given by phone, WhatsApp, email and technician visits at the customer's home or business.
